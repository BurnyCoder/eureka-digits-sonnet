# Parent artifact and semantic review

The controller completed its workflow, and the fresh public clone passed phase verification and checkpoint/prediction regeneration before this review. Parent work is outside the model's active clock and does not change consumed calls, elapsed time, measurements, checkpoints, the protocol or training source.

The first research attempt reported both Sonnet and Haiku because native background WebFetch summaries used a separate model setting. That failed attempt remains in the budget and execution record. After the adapter repair, accepted attempts reported the pinned Sonnet identity. This is not a pure Sonnet execution from inception; see [the failure receipt](model-isolation-failure.json).

The authored interpretation overstated what differing weights and losses establish. They are consistent with different training trajectories, but alone do not prove the optimizer implementation is correct or rule out every bug. The claims and interpretation now state that limit. The [AdamW documentation](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/generated/torch.optim.AdamW.html) supplies an update rule, not that empirical guarantee.

The model-authored final report called all records test-split records, although the record set includes the validation-only pilot. Generated tables already label the splits correctly. Its statement that the lowest shuffled-label score was merely sampling noise was unsupported by a registered sampling model or uncertainty calculation. That conclusion is withdrawn, without changing or discarding the observation. The control shows low accuracy; it cannot exclude every leakage path or implementation error.

The requirement checklist incorrectly treated controller-owned tasks as inapplicable. README, publication, environment and paper attribution apply to the whole project and are now linked to concrete artifacts. This checklist is an evidence index, not proof of every prose-level policy condition. The model's original final and review reports remain as historical submissions with correction notices.

The parent checked the AdamW paper abstract, the [scikit-learn digits documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.datasets.load_digits.html), [UCI metadata](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/80/optical+recognition+of+handwritten+digits), and [Lones's abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.02497) against the source summaries.
